# 🎾 LTA Coach Studio

> **Interactive Tennis Coaching Scenario Designer & Tactical Board**
> Built according to the official **Lawn Tennis Association (LTA)** coaching framework and the 4-tier Lesson Hourglass model.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Interactive Tennis Court (Surface Selector):**
   - **Wimbledon Grass:** Lawn stripes, natural turf physics, low skid bounce.
   - **Roland Garros Clay:** Red brick dust texture, sliding marks, high heavy spin bounce.
   - **US Open Hard Court:** Iconic royal blue inner court with deep forest green surround.
   - **Australian Open:** Bright Pacific blue double-tone acrylic court.
   - **Indoor Carpet:** High-contrast indoor court surface.

2. **LTA Youth & Adult Court Dimensions:**
   - Standard Full Court (78 ft)
   - LTA Red Stage Mini Court (36 ft)
   - LTA Orange Stage Court (60 ft)

3. **Tactical Vector Drawing Board:**
   - Ball trajectory arrows (flat and heavy topspin loops)
   - Player movement and recovery paths (dashed lines)
   - Coach feed arrows (dotted yellow with ball icon)
   - Colored target landing zones
   - High-resolution PNG export

4. **4-Tier Lesson Structure (Hourglass Model):**
   - **Tier 1: Game Assessment** (Initial game context to diagnose tactical/technical priority)
   - **Tier 2: Demo / Teaching Closed** (Isolated technique, coaching cues, closed feed repetitions)
   - **Tier 3: Progressing Open** (Variability, decision-making, dynamic rallies, constraints)
   - **Tier 4: Game** (Competitive match play, bonus point rules, debrief questions)

5. **Smart LTA Scenario Generator (AI-Powered):**
   - Generates complete accredited 4-stage sessions with tactical cues, diagrams, and progressions for any age, level, and situation.

6. **Print-Ready A4 Coaching Card:**
   - One-click export to clean, formatted A4 PDF coaching sheet for on-court use.

7. **Sound Effects (Web Audio API):**
   - Procedural ball strikes, bounces, and coach whistles synthesized in real-time.
